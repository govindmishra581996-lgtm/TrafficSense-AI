TrafficSense-AI

AI-based traffic monitoring and management solution designed for intelligent traffic enforcement and e-challan support.

Project Overview

TrafficSense-AI is an MBA project focused on using AI-based camera systems and radar technology for detecting traffic violations and supporting automated e-challan generation.

Traffic Violations Covered

- Over Speeding – SVD camera with radar-based speed detection
- Without Helmet – VIDS AI camera
- Without Seat Belt – VIDS AI camera
- Triple Riding – VIDS AI camera

Technologies

- AI-based VIDS Camera
- SVD Camera
- Radar-based Speed Detection
- Traffic Violation Detection
- E-Challan System
- Intelligent Traffic Management

Project Objective

The objective of this project is to understand how AI-based video analytics and radar technology can support automated traffic violation detection, improve road safety, and assist efficient traffic enforcement.
